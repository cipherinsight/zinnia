# Four-way benchmark — canonical numbers (2026-06-11 epoch)

Final re-baseline after the provenance-consolidation plan
(`docs/provenance-consolidation-plan.md`, Steps 0–4 complete at commit
`a240525`). All four sweeps ran in one epoch with the same toolchains
(`~/.nargo/bin/nargo`, `~/.bb/bb`, same Zinnia build), repeats = 3.
Per the versioning rule, never compare these gates/cells against numbers
produced by a different bb build or a pre-Step-0c compiler — the ~20
correctness fixes invalidated all earlier baselines (several "fast" old
numbers were witness-specialized mis-compiles).

## Addendum 2026-06-12c — provenance-driven bounds in the Noir emitter

`bound_analysis` now falls back to ProvenanceTable intervals (segment
hulls on ReadMemory, select recognizers, assert hooks), and the u32
subtraction path is gated on a structural non-negativity proof instead
of the witness sample's sign (witness-independent emission). Full sweep
(`zn_noir_gates_20260612_u32prov.json`): net **-4,416 gates** — 0467/0468
-1,626 each, 0392 -744, 0450 -472 (now 0.065x), 0391/0393 -27; the only
regressions are 0508 +56 / 0507 +50, the honest price of removing the
witness-dependent sub gate. Geomean vs contract-equal HW unchanged at
**0.580**. A Field-arithmetic emission experiment (bounded add/mul/select
chains in Field + promotion casts at compare sinks) was measured and
REJECTED: +~1,000-1,500 gates on the bbox family — on current bb a
checked u32 op beats a Field op plus its share of a truncation cast.
0508's remaining +618 vs HW is algorithmic (count-based bbox state vs
the handwritten flag-scan), not a type-policy gap.
